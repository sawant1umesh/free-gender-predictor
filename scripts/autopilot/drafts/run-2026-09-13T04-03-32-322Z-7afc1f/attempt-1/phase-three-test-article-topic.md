## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Chinese Gender Predictor?
A traditional folk chart based on lunar age and lunar months.

### Is the method medically reliable?
No, it has a 50% accuracy rate equivalent to guessing.

### When can doctors determine baby gender?
From 10 weeks with NIPT blood tests or 18-20 weeks with anatomy ultrasound.

### Does baby heart rate predict gender?
No, clinical studies show fetal heart rate does not reliably indicate sex.
