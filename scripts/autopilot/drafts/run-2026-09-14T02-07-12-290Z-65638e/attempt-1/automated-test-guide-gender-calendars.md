## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Chinese Gender Predictor?
It is a traditional folk chart dating back centuries based on lunar months.

### Is it scientifically accurate?
No, scientific studies indicate accuracy is roughly fifty percent.

### When can doctors determine baby gender reliably?
NIPT blood testing is available from week 10, and ultrasound anatomy scans occur around weeks 18 to 20.

### Should I make nursery purchases based on folk charts?
It is best to wait for clinical ultrasound or NIPT confirmation before making major purchases.
