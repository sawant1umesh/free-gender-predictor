Finding out whether you are expecting a boy or a girl is one of the most thrilling parts of pregnancy. Today's parents have access to both ancient cultural traditions and cutting-edge medical technology.

This guide compares popular traditional gender prediction methods against clinical medical diagnostics.

## Traditional Gender Prediction Methods

For centuries, parents used observational and calendar-based methods:

### 1. Chinese Gender Predictor
Uses maternal lunar age and lunar conception month on a 700-year-old Qing Dynasty chart.
- **Accuracy:** ~50%

### 2. Mayan Gender Predictor
Uses even and odd parity of maternal age and conception month.
- **Accuracy:** ~50%

### 3. Old Wives' Tales (Belly Shape, Heart Rate, Ring Test)
Claims high vs. low belly carrying, fetal heart rates above 140 bpm, or wedding ring pendulum swings predict gender.
- **Accuracy:** ~50%

## Clinical Medical Testing

Only verified medical procedures can accurately determine fetal sex:

| Medical Test | Timing | Accuracy | How It Works |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NIPT Blood Test** | 10+ Weeks | > 99% | Detects Y-chromosome cell-free DNA in maternal blood. |
| **Ultrasound Scan** | 18–20 Weeks | > 99% | Visually identifies physical fetal genitalia. |
| **CVS / Amniocentesis** | 10–20 Weeks | 100% | Karyotypes fetal chromosomes from tissue/fluid. |

## Conclusion

Enjoy traditional prediction tools like the [Chinese Gender Predictor](https://freegenderpredictor.com) as a lighthearted early pregnancy activity, but rely on your OB/GYN and medical imaging for definitive answers.
